% Section 5: Discussion and Limitations
\paragraph{What the decomposition shows.}
The headline result is that ordinary spatial fine-tuning is not a scalar
capability update. Benchmark accuracy, correct-image dependence, and
semantic pair consistency move together in direction (all positive for
\dA{} and \dG{}; \dC{} stable) but they are distinct quantities: shuffle
accuracy stays flat while the gap widens; answer-update rates improve
while transformed accuracy moves differently; and the three Tier-C
quantities never collapse into one number. A model can get the mirrored
item right without updating its answer relative to its own original
prediction, and vice versa; reporting them separately is essential.

\paragraph{Seed robustness.}
Every component replicates across three fresh training draws per family.
The fresh-seed standard deviations are small (7B \dA{} SD 0.0046; 2B \dA{}
SD 0.0009), which closes the single-checkpoint-anecdote criticism that a
training effect could be a lucky draw.

\paragraph{Relationship to prior work.}
We do not claim the normal-minus-shuffle gap as novel: VRS in Beyond
Accuracy~\cite{zafar2026beyond} is the same construction. We do not claim
to reproduce VisualFLIP~\cite{zhu2026visualflip}: our reflection is a
global image intervention, and we state the difference. Our novelty is the
joint, seed-robust, cross-backbone decomposition of ordinary fine-tuning
into \dA{}, \dG{}, \dC{}, and Tier-C behavior.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
(1) Two backbone families (7B and 2B) plus one contemporary extension; the
extension computed only \dA{}/\dG{}/$A_{\text{transform}}$ and is
exploratory. (2) The Tier-C intervention is a global horizontal
reflection; VisualFLIP-style minimal local edits were not run because the
official dataset is not yet public. (3) All comparisons are within-backbone
with identical preprocessing; we do not claim resolution or
architecture independence. (4) The shuffle condition tests dependence on
the \emph{correct image}, which we call correct-image dependence, not
``causal grounding''; stronger causal language is reserved for genuine
counterfactual evidence. (5) A text-only control adapter was considered
but not run; it remains future work. (6) Seeds are independent draws; we
do not claim a ``monotonic'' seed trend anywhere.
