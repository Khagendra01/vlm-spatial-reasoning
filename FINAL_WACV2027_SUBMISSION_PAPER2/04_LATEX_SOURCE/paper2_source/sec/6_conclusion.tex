% Section 6: Conclusion
Ordinary spatial LoRA fine-tuning on VSR produces a reproducible vector of
capability changes, not a single benchmark gain. Across three fresh
training seeds and two backbone families, \dA{} (benchmark accuracy) and
\dG{} (correct-image dependence) are positive for every seed, semantic
pair consistency \dC{} stays within 0.05 of the legacy General checkpoint,
and Tier-C transformation behavior improves answer-update rates while
remaining a set of three separately reported quantities. A contemporary
\qwennew{} extension reproduces the primary \dA{}/\dG{} pattern and the
transformed-accuracy gain. Every number was independently recomputed from
raw artifacts and the frozen protocol; the full claim hierarchy,
numerical audit, literature audit, figures, and tables are archived in the
repository. We recommend that spatial fine-tuning be evaluated and
reported as this joint vector---accuracy, correct-image dependence,
semantic consistency, and transformation behavior---rather than collapsed
into a single accuracy number.
