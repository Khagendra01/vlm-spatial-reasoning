% Section 2: Related Work
\paragraph{Accuracy versus visual grounding.}
A central and rapidly growing observation is that benchmark accuracy can
overstate genuine visual dependence. \emph{Beyond Accuracy}
(Zafar et al., 2026)~\cite{zafar2026beyond} evaluates medical VLMs with
real, blank, and shuffled images and defines the Visual Reliance Score
(VRS) as $A(\text{real}) - A(\text{shuffle})$; they find that RLVR can
improve accuracy while degrading visual grounding. Our \emph{correct-image
dependence} (the normal-minus-shuffle gap, $G$) is the same construction;
we do not claim it as novel, and we report the zero-shot-relative change
\dG{} as the adaptation quantity. \emph{Seeing without Looking}
(Lan et al., 2026)~\cite{lan2026seeing} applies global and localized
visual interventions to show that predictions can remain robust after
evidence removal; it audits pretrained models rather than a fine-tuning
transition. \emph{VisualFLIP} (Zhu et al., 2026)~\cite{zhu2026visualflip}
introduces a paired benchmark where the question stays fixed and a minimal
task-critical edit deterministically flips the gold answer, with pair
accuracy and Collapse Rate as metrics; its dataset and harness are not yet
publicly released, and we do not claim to reproduce its protocol. Our
Tier-C horizontal reflection is a global image intervention, not a minimal
local edit; we report our pair metrics as \emph{collapse-style paired
answer-update metrics following VisualFLIP}, with the intervention
difference stated explicitly.

\paragraph{Consistency versus grounding.}
\emph{Consistent Yet Wrong} (Bhat and Yamasaki, 2026)~\cite{bhat2026consistent}
introduces ViewDiag and shows that multi-view consistency can coexist with
substantial error, indicating prior-driven collapse rather than
evidence-sensitive reasoning. Their dissociation between consistency and
grounding is the static analogue of our \dC{}-versus-\dG{} decomposition,
but they do not study a fine-tuning transition. \emph{VisualFLIP}'s
collapse rate is a similar paired-consistency notion for minimal-edit
pairs.

\paragraph{Grounding-aware training objectives.}
\emph{CORAL} (Zafar et al., 2026)~\cite{zafar2026coral} fine-tunes
Qwen2.5-VL-7B with a Contrastive Grounding Objective that penalizes answer
invariance under hard-negative image swaps, improving accuracy and
reducing visual hallucination in medical VQA. \emph{Med-OPD}
(Qian et al., 2026)~\cite{qian2026medopd} adds evidence-aware on-policy
distillation for medical VLMs. These works \emph{design} grounding-aware
objectives; we study what \emph{ordinary} fine-tuning already changes,
without modifying the objective.

\paragraph{Positioning.}
No prior work, to our knowledge, treats ordinary spatial task fine-tuning
as a controlled intervention and measures \dA{}, \dG{}, and \dC{} (plus
Tier-C transformation behavior) jointly, per training seed, across two
backbone families, with a contemporary-backbone extension. Our novelty
claim is precisely this joint, seed-robust decomposition---not the
normal-minus-shuffle gap, not ``accuracy differs from grounding,'' and not
a new training objective.
