\section{Depth probe: n, intervals, and the z\_d block}

Regime B: probe fit on validation-scene depth pairs (identity images),
scored on hold-out-scene depth pairs, $n{=}60$ (3 scenes $\times$ 20
pairs); Wilson 95\% CI for 0.5333 (32/60) is [0.41, 0.65]; chance 0.5.
All arms are inside the interval. The $\zblk_d$-block-only probe
(0.52--0.55) is likewise flat, so the invariance-shaped depth component
did not acquire decodable depth structure.

Regime A: $n{=}36$; EquiOrient 0.5000, augmentation 0.6111 (22/36,
Wilson CI [0.45, 0.75]) -- within noise of each other and of chance.
