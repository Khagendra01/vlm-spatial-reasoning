\section{Bootstrap analysis}

Hierarchical paired bootstrap: resample training seeds, then test
scene IDs within each seed, preserving method pairing.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item Mean $\Delta A$ (EquiOrient $-$ Augmentation): $0.0000$ (at $N{=}512$, all ceiling)
\item 95\% CI: $[0.0000, 0.0000]$
\item Individual seed deltas: all exactly $0.0000$ at $N \geq 512$
\item Minimum meaningful effect: $0.03$ (3pp)
\item Conclusion: no evidence of meaningful behavioral benefit at $N \geq 512$
\end{itemize}

Predeclared paired contrasts:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{EquiOrient $-$ Augmentation:} $-0.93$pp, 95\% CI
$[-2.28, +0.46]$pp, paired $t$-test $p=0.215$, wins $5/15$ seeds.
Not significant.
\item \textbf{EquiOrient $-$ Wrong geometry:} $+0.68$pp, 95\% CI
$[+0.49, +0.89]$pp, paired $t$-test $p<0.0001$, wins $15/15$ seeds.
Significant: behavior distinguishes the correct law from the
incorrect one.
\end{itemize}

At $N{=}128$ the low-data regime does not ceiling: correct structural
supervision does not outperform unconstrained augmentation, but
behavior is reliably sensitive to whether the imposed geometric law
is correct. At $N \geq 512$ all of these contrasts vanish at ceiling.
