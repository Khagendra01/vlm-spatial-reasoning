## SITE secondary-subset discrepancy (47.3 vs 48.3) — RESOLVED (analysis bug, corrected)

Root cause: an earlier derived analysis (`compare_site_zeroshot_lora.py` v1,
which also fed the initial canonical site table and figure) reconstructed the
secondary subset by applying `ORIENTATION_KEYWORDS` to the **question text
only**, yielding n = 1,824 (47.3% / 22.6%). The preregistered definition is
`results/site/site_protocol.json` → `frozen_ids.secondary` (3,272 total; 2,024
image IDs), and the prediction CSVs' `subset` column (written from those frozen
IDs at eval time) tags exactly 2,024 rows secondary.

**Resolution (no inference rerun, no CSV modification):** the frozen-ID
definition is authoritative and is now used everywhere:

| Subset (frozen IDs ∩ evaluated) | n | Zero-shot raw (CAA) | VSR-LoRA raw (CAA) | Δ | McNemar p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All images | 2,591 | 54.2 (31.1) | 53.8 (30.5) | −0.4 | 0.66 |
| Primary (official spatial-rel.) | 993 | 75.1 (59.2) | 71.6 (53.4) | −3.5 | **0.004** |
| Secondary (orientation kw.) | **2,024** | **48.3 (24.0)** | **48.7 (24.5)** | +0.4 | **0.70** |
| Exploratory (movement & nav.) | 248 | 48.8 (23.1) | 50.4 (25.6) | +1.6 | 0.64 |
| single-image | 1,368 | 61.3 (37.4) | 60.2 (35.6) | −1.1 | 0.33 |
| multi-image | 1,223 | 46.4 (24.9) | 46.8 (25.5) | +0.4 | 0.78 |

Notes: (i) the zero-shot secondary value was previously reported two ways
(metrics JSON 47.3 vs CSV 48.3); the 1,824 figure is now explained as the
question-only reconstruction, and the frozen-ID path (2,024 / 48.3 / 24.0) is
the single reported value. (ii) The LoRA secondary p-value **changed from 0.50
(question-only, n=1,824) to 0.70 (frozen-ID, n=2,024)** — the conclusion (no
significant transfer) is unchanged, and the primary-category negative transfer
(p=0.004) is unaffected. (iii) All other SITE numbers were bit-identical across
both artifact paths and remain unchanged. (iv) A short audit note in the
supplementary (§E) records the correction; the main-text discrepancy footnote
was removed. (v) The old 47.3/1,824 numbers survive only in git history and in
`zeroshot_image_metrics.json` (kept as the run-time record).
